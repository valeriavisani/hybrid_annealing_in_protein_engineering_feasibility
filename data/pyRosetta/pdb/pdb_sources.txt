SOURCES OF THE PDB FILES:

All instances valutate nel paper were built from experimentally determined structures retrieved from
the RCSB Protein Data Bank (PDB).

This document report the name of the files, the PDB ID and the link to the RCSB Protein Data Bank page.

In some structures, residues with missing atoms were removed; consequently, the number of residues of
these instances is slightly smaller than the target size. We also note that the 10-residue structure
(PDB ID 138D) is a DNA decamer rather than a protein. In this case Rosetta provides four conformational
states for each position, corresponding to the four nucleotides, which serve a role analogous to that
of rotamers for the purposes of our calculations.
